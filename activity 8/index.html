STADIUM PROTOCOLS

Stadium Guidelines & Code of Conduct

Please adhere to the official BCCI stadium directives for a safe and hassle-free matchday experience.

📱
Protocol 01

Digital QR Verification

Present your high-contrast digital QR e-ticket directly on your mobile device at entrance turnstiles. Keep device brightness elevated for rapid automated contactless barcode validation.

  • Save PDF offline before arriving
  • Single turnstile scan per attendee
🪪
Protocol 02

Government Photo ID Required

Each ticket holder must carry a valid original Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Passport, or Voter ID Card). Name on physical ID must match reservation credentials.

  • Original physical ID or DigiLocker copy
  • Random security gate spot-checks
🚫
Protocol 03

Security & Prohibited Items

Outside food, glass/metal beverage bottles, power banks, umbrellas with pointed ends, helmets, professional cameras with detachable telephoto lenses, and flammable items are strictly prohibited.

  • Free baggage counters at Gate 4
  • Full bag screening prior to gate access
